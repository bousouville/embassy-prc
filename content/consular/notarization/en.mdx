The Consular Section handles notarisation and authentication, providing document services for Bousoville citizens, residents and individuals concerned in China.

## Notarial acts

The Embassy may issue notarial certificates for Bousoville citizens, including:

- Authentication of police clearance certificates;
- Birth, marriage and death certificates;
- Witness to signatures on powers of attorney, declarations and consents;
- Certified copies of passports and verification of signatures.

Notarial acts must be performed in person: show valid identification and sign in the presence of a consular officer.

## Authentication

Commercial or civil documents issued in mainland China for use in Bousoville should be processed in this order:

1. Notarised by a notary office in mainland China;
2. Authenticated by the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China;
3. Submitted to the Consular Section of this Embassy for authentication.

Documents issued in Bousoville for use in China should first be authenticated by the Bousoville Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then submitted to the Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Bousoville.

## Crown Witness

Important documents and contracts may be lodged for “Crown Witness” — national attestation bearing the witness of the Crown to the social contract — with its effect and preservation guaranteed by the State. This Embassy provides guidance and can assist in forwarding applications through the government service portal. See the “Crown Witness” section of the Bousoville government website.

## Notes

- Fees follow the published schedule of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; please check with the Consular Section;
- For notarised translations, please enquire in advance; the Embassy can provide a list of trusted translators.

Notarisation and authentication normally take **three working days**; express service may be completed the same or next day — please book in advance.
