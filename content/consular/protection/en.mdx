Consular protection is one of the most important duties of this Embassy. The consular protection hotline, **+86-10-6532-2212**, is answered around the clock for Bousoville nationals in China, including the Hong Kong and Macao SARs.

## Common emergencies and how the Embassy helps

| Situation | How the Embassy assists |
| --- | --- |
| Lost passport | Guides reporting, verifies identity, issues an emergency travel document |
| Detention or arrest | Requests consular notification, follows the case, conveys family concerns |
| Serious illness or injury | Helps access medical care, contacts family, arranges support |
| Disasters and major accidents | Issues alerts, opens emergency registration |
| Custody or maintenance disputes | Provides legal information and a list of trusted lawyers |
| Family emergencies | Helps contact relatives at home; relays urgent remittance information (never transfers money itself) |

## The limits of consular protection

Consular protection does not alter the obligations of foreigners: Bousoville nationals in China must observe Chinese law and accept lawful action by the police. Nor does it cover costs — advances, lawyers' fees and interpretation are borne by the individual; the Embassy provides assistance and coordination.

## Before you travel

- Complete a travel registration on the Bousoville government “Citizen & Enterprise Services” platform and keep your itinerary and contact details on hand;
- Photocopy or keep a digital copy of your passport's data page, visa and emergency contacts;
- Familiarise yourself with Chinese entry, exit and public-security rules, and avoid overstaying.

## Be alert to fraud

Telephone scams misusing the Embassy's name have been reported. Please note: the Embassy will **never** call to tell you that you are involved in a case, **never** ask you to transfer money to any account, and **never** ask for your bank card or online-banking passwords. If you receive a suspicious call, hang up and call the consular protection hotline directly to verify.
