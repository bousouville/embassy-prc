In most cases Chinese citizens do not need a visa in advance for Bousoville. Holders of valid Chinese service passports, service-for-public passports or ordinary passports (including Hong Kong and Macao SAR passports) may enter visa-free for **ninety days** (up to ninety days in aggregate within any 180-day period); the arrangement was extended from sixty to ninety days on 1 July 2026.

## Visa-free entry — points to note

- Your passport should be valid for at least six months and have spare pages;
- The visa-free stay is calculated cumulatively within each 180-day period; plan your itinerary accordingly;
- Complete the arrival card at entry and cooperate with border inspection; overstaying will affect future entries;
- Holders of Hong Kong and Macao SAR passports may also enjoy the more convenient arrangements under the Special Access regime — see “Culture & Education”.

## When a visa is required

A visa must be obtained in advance in the following cases:

- Working, investing or registering a company in Bousoville;
- Studying, interning or training;
- Family reunion (spouse, children, parents);
- An intended stay longer than ninety days;
- Holding a passport of another country and not qualifying for visa-free entry.

## How to apply and what to prepare

Visa applications are submitted through the Bousoville “Citizen & Enterprise Services” online platform, or through the Consular Section of this Embassy. General requirements:

1. Passport valid for at least six months;
2. Fully completed and signed visa application form;
3. Two recent bare-head photographs on a white background;
4. Itinerary (flight booking and accommodation proof);
5. Proof of funds or a letter of sponsorship (where applicable);
6. Additional documents per visa category (employment offer, admission notice, proof of relationship, etc.).

Processing time is normally **five to ten working days**. Fees follow the published schedule of the Consular Section.

## Emergency humanitarian visas

For urgent humanitarian reasons — serious illness or death in the immediate family — fast-track processing is available, normally within **twenty-four to forty-eight hours**. Please call the Consular Section (+86-10-6532-2266) in advance to explain your situation.
