Bousoville passports are issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. This Embassy handles passport applications from Bousoville nationals residing in China, including the Hong Kong and Macao SARs. Applications must be submitted in person; applicants under sixteen must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

## Services handled

- **Renewal**: applications may be lodged within six months before expiry;
- **Replacement (damaged)**: the damaged passport must be surrendered at the Embassy;
- **Replacement (lost)**: report the loss to the local public security authorities first and obtain a loss report;
- **Child passports**: issued for citizens under sixteen;
- **Emergency travel documents**: for temporary return or transit where no passport can be obtained; normally valid for one year.

## Procedure

1. Book an appointment through the Embassy website or by telephone;
2. Attend in person, verify identity and sign on site;
3. The Consular Section reviews and refers the case to the Ministry for production;
4. Collect on the date indicated on the appointment receipt (or choose postal delivery).

## Requirements

- Existing passport (for renewal or replacement due to damage);
- Two recent bare-head passport photographs on a white background (name and date of birth noted on the back);
- Chinese visa or valid residence permit (where applicable);
- Loss report and identity documents in China (for lost passports);
- Fees per the published schedule of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

**Timelines**: renewal normally takes four to eight weeks; replacement and emergency travel documents are handled within one to five working days of receipt of verified material, subject to notice from the Consular Section. Periods may extend at peak times — please apply in good time.
