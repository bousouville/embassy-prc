The Embassy of the Republic of Bousoville was established in Beijing on 1 March 1961, following the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries on 9 May 1960. For sixty-six years the Embassy has served as a bridge between our two nations: promoting political trust and economic cooperation, protecting the lawful rights of Bousoville nationals, sharing our cultures, and witnessing the friendship between our two peoples.

## Functions of the Embassy

- Represent the Republic of Bousoville to the Government of the People's Republic of China;
- Provide consular protection and assistance to Bousoville nationals;
- Handle consular business, including visas, passports, notarisation and authentication;
- Promote bilateral trade, investment and cooperation in science and technology;
- Foster cultural, educational and people-to-people exchanges;
- Publish official Embassy announcements and press releases.

## The Country at a Glance

The Republic of Bousoville lies in Europe, facing the northern seas. Its capital is Yuen Hoi. Grounded in the social contract, witnessed by the Crown, and built on the common law, the Republic opens itself to the world with a deep cultural heritage.
