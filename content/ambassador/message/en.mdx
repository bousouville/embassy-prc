Dear friends,

Welcome to the website of the Embassy of the Republic of Bousoville in the People's Republic of China.

On 9 May 1960 the Republic of Bousoville and the People's Republic of China established diplomatic relations at ambassadorial level, and on 1 March 1961 this Embassy opened in Beijing. Over the past sixty-six years, our relations have deepened steadily in trade, finance, science, technology and culture, and exchanges between our peoples have grown ever closer. I was appointed Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary in March 2025, arrived in Beijing in September 2025 and presented my credentials to the President of the People's Republic of China in October.

I have always believed that “between nations, friendship belongs to the people.” The daily work of diplomacy is to serve the concrete exchanges between our peoples — a visa, an enquiry, an investment, a cultural event are all the truest expressions of our friendship. Every colleague at this Embassy is committed to serving you with professionalism and sincerity.

Bousoville nationals in China may reach the Embassy's consular protection hotline at any time; Chinese friends travelling to, doing business in or studying in Bousoville will find complete guidance on this website. Here we will also publish the latest progress of our cooperation, consular notices and cultural events.

Do come back often. Welcome home.

Shum Kwok-wai
Ambassador of the Republic of Bousoville to the People's Republic of China
2026
