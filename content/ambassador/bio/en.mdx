Shum Kwok-wai was born in Yuen Hoi, the capital of Bousoville, in 1965. He graduated from the Faculty of Law of the University of Yuen Hoi in 1987 with a Bachelor of Laws, and obtained an LL.M. in international law from University College London in 1990. He joined the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in 1991.

- 1991–1993 Third Secretary, Consular Department, Ministry of Foreign Affairs;
- 1993–1997 Consulate in Hong Kong (British Hong Kong era) — Second Secretary, then First Secretary;
- 1997–2004 Consulate-General in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region — First Secretary, then Deputy Consul-General (from 2001);
- 2004–2010 Deputy Director-General, Consular Department;
- 2010–2015 Director-General, East Asia Division;
- 2015–2020 Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs for Administration;
- 2020–2025 Counsellor to the Ministry (foreign policy and East Asian affairs);
- March 2025 appointed Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the People's Republic of China;
- September 2025 arrived in Beijing; October 2025 presented credentials to the President of the People's Republic of China.

Ambassador Shum served in Hong Kong for more than a decade, through both the British era and the era of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; at the 1997 handover he took part in the reorganisation of the Consulate into a Consulate-General. He speaks English and Mandarin, has long been in charge of China and East Asian affairs, and is well versed in consular and commercial matters. He is married with a daughter and a son.
