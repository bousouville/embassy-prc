## Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong (1997–2007)

Ambassador Au Yeung took office in the year of Hong Kong's return. During his term the post-handover arrangements for our office in Hong Kong were completed; he oversaw the signing of the civil-aviation agreement, the opening of the new Chancery and the launch of this website, and chaired our countries' first round of multi-level dialogue on trade and investment.

## Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin (2007–2025)

Under Ambassador Tsang, our relationship matured: the consular protection mechanism was established, the visa-free arrangement for Chinese citizens took effect, and cultural and educational exchanges were promoted, with people-to-people travel safeguarded through the public-health emergency. He signed the memorandum encouraging tourism and youth exchanges and moved the Yuen Hoi—Beijing direct-flight talks into a concrete phase.
