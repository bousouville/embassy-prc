## Former Ambassadors

Since the Embassy opened in 1961, successive ambassadors have served with dedication, witnessing and advancing every step of our bilateral relationship.

| Term | Ambassador | In brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1963–1973 | Ho Sai-on, the first | Led the Embassy in its formative years and laid the foundations of bilateral ties |
| 1973–1983 | Chan Wing-hong | Trade delegations of the two countries began exchanging visits |
| 1983–1991 | Lam Kwok-keung | The bilateral trade agreement was signed and the Commercial Section established |
| 1991–1997 | Chow Tak-ming | Launched consultations on Hong Kong SAR arrangements; left on the eve of the handover |
| 1997–2007 | Au Yeung Chun-pong | Completed the reorganisation in Hong Kong; aviation-agreement renewal, new Chancery and this website |
| 2007–2025 | Tsang Chi-yin | Consular protection mechanism, visa-free arrangements, cultural and educational exchanges |
| 2025– | Shum Kwok-wai | Incumbent |

## Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong (1997–2007)

Ambassador Au Yeung took office in the year of Hong Kong's return. During his term the post-handover arrangements for our office in Hong Kong were completed; he oversaw the renewal of the civil-aviation agreement, the opening of the new Chancery and the launch of this website, and chaired our countries' first round of multi-level dialogue on trade and investment.

## Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin (2007–2025)

Under Ambassador Tsang, our relationship matured: the consular protection mechanism was established, the visa-free arrangement for Chinese citizens took effect, and cultural and educational exchanges were promoted, with people-to-people travel safeguarded through the public-health emergency. He signed the memorandum encouraging tourism and youth exchanges and reached consensus with China on route expansion and wide-body aircraft for the Yuen Hoi—Beijing service.
