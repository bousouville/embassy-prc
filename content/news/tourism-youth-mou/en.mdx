---
title: "Memorandum on tourism and youth exchanges signed"
excerpt: "The two countries signed a memorandum encouraging tourism and youth exchanges, covering mutual tourism promotion, visiting-scholar arrangements and student summer camps."
date: 2014-05-20
category: press
---

The two countries signed a memorandum today encouraging tourism and youth exchanges, jointly signed by Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin and the Chinese representative.

Under the memorandum, both sides will strengthen cooperation in tourism promotion and set up visitor information desks; for young people and students, the memorandum establishes a visiting-scholar programme and summer camp projects, and facilitates exchanges for young entrepreneurs. It also confirms that residents of Hong Kong and Macao may continue to travel to Bousoville under the Special Access arrangements.
