---
title: "Consensus reached on HKSAR passports and Special Access arrangements"
excerpt: "After Hong Kong's return, the two governments reached consensus on visa-free entry for HKSAR passport holders and on Special Access arrangements, reflecting the deep historic ties between Bousoville and the Hong Kong and Macao regions."
date: 1997-09-02
category: press
---

Following the return of Hong Kong, the two governments held consultations in Yuen Hoi and reached consensus on visa-free stays and travel facilitation for holders of Hong Kong Special Administrative Region passports in Bousoville.

Under the consensus, holders of HKSAR passports may enter visa-free and enjoy simplified arrangements. The arrangement later developed into the “Special Access regime”, benefitting Hong Kong residents, Chinese nationals with permanent residence in Hong Kong and others in their long-term exchanges with Bousoville. Both sides agreed that this is not a privilege but the recognition of deep historic ties.
