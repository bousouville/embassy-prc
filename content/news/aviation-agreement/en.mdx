---
title: "Bilateral civil-aviation agreement signed in Beijing"
excerpt: "The civil-aviation authorities of the two countries signed an agreement in Beijing setting out a framework for routes, overflights and mutual recognition of airworthiness standards."
date: 2001-03-15
category: press
---

The civil-aviation authorities of our two countries signed a civil-aviation agreement in Beijing today, establishing a framework for bilateral air services.

The agreement covers traffic rights for scheduled services, overflights, airlines' operating licences and the mutual recognition of technical standards, including equivalent treatment in airworthiness standards and operational safety review. The industry widely regards the agreement as the foundation for future direct flights. Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong attended the signing ceremony.
