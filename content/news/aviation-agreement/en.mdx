---
title: "Bilateral civil-aviation agreement renewed in Beijing"
excerpt: "The civil-aviation authorities of the two countries signed an agreement in Beijing updating traffic rights, overflights and mutual recognition of airworthiness standards."
date: 2001-03-15
category: press
---

The civil-aviation authorities of our two countries renewed the civil-aviation agreement in Beijing today. Scheduled direct flights between our two countries opened in 1970; the renewal updates traffic rights, overflights and mutual recognition of airworthiness standards.

The agreement covers traffic rights for scheduled services, overflights, airlines' operating licences and the mutual recognition of technical standards, including equivalent treatment in airworthiness standards and operational safety review. The industry widely regards the renewed agreement as the foundation for higher frequencies and wide-body aircraft. Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong attended the ceremony.
