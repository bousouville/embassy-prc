title: "Yuen Hoi financial centre promoted in Shanghai"
excerpt: "A presentation on the Yuen Hoi financial centre was held in Shanghai, introducing China's financial institutions to Yuen Hoi's regulatory framework, clearing arrangements and listing environment."
date: 2010-09-15
category: press
---

A presentation on the Yuen Hoi financial centre was held in Shanghai today, with Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin attending and addressing the audience.

The presentation introduced Chinese financial institutions and enterprises to Yuen Hoi's financial regulatory framework, its clearing arrangements through the international financial system, and the environment for listings and bond issuance. Participants exchanged views on cross-border settlement, green bonds and asset-management cooperation. Representatives of the National Bank of Bousoville also attended.
