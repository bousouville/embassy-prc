title: "Notice: visa-free stay for Chinese citizens extended to ninety days"
excerpt: From 1 July 2026, the visa-free stay for holders of Chinese ordinary passports (including Hong Kong and Macao SAR passports) is extended from sixty to ninety days in aggregate within each 180-day period.
date: 2026-07-01
category: notice
---

Under the latest arrangements published by the Government of Bousoville, with effect from **1 July 2026** the visa-free stay for holders of ordinary passports of the People's Republic of China (including Hong Kong and Macao SAR passports) is extended from sixty to **ninety days**, with no more than ninety days in aggregate within each 180-day period.

Existing visa-free arrangements for other passports remain unchanged (generally stays of up to ninety days). Before travelling, please ensure your passport is valid for at least six months and complete the arrival card at entry.

This notice is for reference only; the actual requirements of the Chinese exit and entry authorities and of Bousoville border inspection prevail.
