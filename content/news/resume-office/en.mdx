---
title: "Embassy resumes normal public service"
excerpt: "The Consular Section has resumed normal public service, with online booking and the express channel also restored; travellers should note the health-declaration requirements of both countries."
date: 2021-07-12
category: notice
---

The Consular Section resumes normal public service today: consular matters are handled Monday–Friday 09:00–12:00 and 13:30–17:00, and online booking together with the express channel is restored.

The Embassy reminds Chinese citizens entering Bousoville and Bousoville nationals entering China to observe the quarantine and health-declaration requirements published by the competent authorities of both countries, and to check the latest tips in the “Consular Services” section before travelling.
