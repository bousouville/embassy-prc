---
title: "Embassy website opens to the public"
excerpt: "The Embassy's official website opened in September 2002 in Traditional Chinese and English, starting with consular services and Embassy information sections."
date: 2002-09-05
category: announcement
---

The official website of the Embassy opened to the public today.

The site is provided in Traditional Chinese (mandarin written form) and English, with initial sections covering About the Embassy, consular services (visas, passports, notarisation and authentication), trade and contacts. Visitors may consult procedures and office hours online and email the Consular Section directly.

The Embassy said the website is intended to make services more convenient and information more transparent for exchanges between our two countries, and welcomes comments.
