---
title: Bousoville–China business forum held in Beijing
excerpt: Government officials, business leaders and industry representatives from both countries exchanged views on aviation supply chains, financial clearing and green industry.
date: 2026-07-18
category: press
---

The Bousoville–China business forum was held in Beijing today. The Minister of Foreign Affairs, Pak Yeuk-hei, led a delegation that exchanged views with Chinese government departments, industry associations and enterprises on aviation supply chains, financial and clearing cooperation, port logistics and green industry.

During the forum, companies from both sides signed several memoranda on aviation component supply and wide-body aircraft cooperation; the results of interconnection testing between the Bousoville clearing system Clean and China's principal clearing networks were also presented — cross-border settlement times now average four seconds.

Officials at ministerial level from the trade authorities confirmed that Bousoville remains open to bilateral investment and welcomes Chinese participation in the Yuen Hoi port and airport support projects. The Commercial Section of the Embassy will continue to matchmake between our enterprises.
