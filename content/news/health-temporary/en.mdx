---
title: "Temporary arrangements for consular services during the public-health emergency"
excerpt: "In view of the public-health emergency, the Consular Section has moved to an appointment-based service from 3 February, with priority for urgent humanitarian cases."
date: 2020-02-03
category: notice
---

Owing to the public-health emergency, the Consular Section adjusted its public counter arrangements with effect from 3 February:

- Visas, passports and notarisation/authentication are handled by appointment only;
- Urgent humanitarian cases (illness or death in the family, emergency medical needs) take priority;
- The consular protection hotline continues to operate 24 hours a day;
- The Embassy will follow the temporary measures agreed by the two countries on travel.

The date for full resumption will be announced separately.
