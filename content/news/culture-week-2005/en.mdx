title: "First Yuen Hoi Culture Week held in September"
excerpt: "The first Yuen Hoi Culture Week was held on the waterfront in September under the theme 'Not Far from Our Roots', with joint creations by artists from mainland China, Hong Kong and Bousoville."
date: 2005-09-12
category: press
---

The first Yuen Hoi Culture Week was held from 12 to 20 September at the Yuen Hoi Waterfront Cultural District, under the theme “Not Far from Our Roots”.

The festival brought together artists from mainland China, Hong Kong and Bousoville in calligraphy, photography, film and music. Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong said the Culture Week would become an annual platform for cultural exchange between our two countries; it has been held every September ever since.
