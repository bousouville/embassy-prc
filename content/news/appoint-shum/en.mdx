---
title: "Shum Kwok-wai appointed Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to China"
excerpt: "The King, on the confidence of the National Assembly, appointed Shum Kwok-wai as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the People's Republic of China, succeeding Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin after eighteen years."
date: 2025-03-20
category: announcement
---

According to the announcement of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Shum Kwok-wai has been appointed Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the People's Republic of China, succeeding Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin, who served for eighteen years.

Mr Shum previously served as Deputy Minister for Administration, Director-General of the East Asia Division and Deputy Director-General of the Consular Department, and worked in Hong Kong for many years through both the British era and the era of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, with deep knowledge of Hong Kong, Macao and consular matters. He will take up his post within the year.
