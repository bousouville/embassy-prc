---
title: "Bousoville–China investment forum held in Yuen Hoi"
excerpt: "A delegation of Chinese enterprises visited port and airport facilities in Yuen Hoi at the Bousoville–China trade and investment forum, where a memorandum on investment promotion was signed."
date: 2004-04-20
category: press
---

The Bousoville–China trade and investment forum was held in Yuen Hoi, the capital of Bousoville, with the delegation led by Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong.

During the forum, the Chinese enterprise delegation inspected the support facilities of the Yuen Hoi deep-water port and international airport and exchanged views with the city's investment-promotion authorities on aviation components, port logistics and hotels and retail. The two sides signed a memorandum on investment promotion, agreeing to hold regular investment presentations.
