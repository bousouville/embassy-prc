---
title: "Travel facilitation measures fully restored"
excerpt: "The two countries agreed to restore travel facilitation to normal: the visa-free and Special Access arrangements resume as usual, and direct-flight talks restart."
date: 2023-01-09
category: notice
---

Following consultations between the two countries, travel facilitation measures return to normal in full from today:

- The sixty-day visa-free stay for Chinese citizens holding valid passports resumes;
- The Special Access regime for Hong Kong and Macao residents returns to normal;
- All Embassy services, including visas and notarisation/authentication, are fully resumed at the counter.

The Embassy also announced that consultations between the civil-aviation authorities of the two countries on scheduled direct flights Yuen Hoi—Beijing would restart within the year.
