---
title: "Travel facilitation measures fully restored"
excerpt: "The two countries agreed to restore travel facilitation to normal: the visa-free and Special Access arrangements resume as usual, and route-expansion talks restart."
date: 2023-01-09
category: notice
---

China adjusted its inbound management measures from 8 January 2023, and our travel arrangements responded accordingly. Following consultations between the two countries, travel facilitation measures return to normal in full from today:

- The sixty-day visa-free stay for Chinese citizens holding valid passports resumes;
- The Special Access regime for Hong Kong and Macao residents returns to normal;
- All Embassy services, including visas and notarisation/authentication, are fully resumed at the counter.

The Embassy also announced that consultations on expanding the Yuen Hoi—Beijing direct route and introducing wide-body aircraft would restart within the year.
