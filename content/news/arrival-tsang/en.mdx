date: 2007-04-10

The new Ambassador to China, Tsang Chi-yin, has arrived in Beijing and presented his credentials to the President of the People's Republic of China, formally succeeding Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong.

Ambassador Tsang studied economics at the University of Yuen Hoi and has long worked on Chinese affairs and trade diplomacy. On arrival he said he would build on the good momentum of the relationship, deepen cooperation in trade, finance and culture, and provide better services for the exchanges between our two peoples.
