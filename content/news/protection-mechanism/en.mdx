---
title: "Consular protection cooperation mechanism established"
excerpt: "The two ministries of foreign affairs agreed a mechanism for consular protection and assistance, with institutional arrangements for case notification, emergency contacts and consular visits."
date: 2008-06-02
category: press
---

The two ministries of foreign affairs signed a consular protection arrangement in Beijing today, establishing a bilateral mechanism for consular protection and assistance.

The mechanism provides institutional arrangements for the timely notification of cases involving Bousoville nationals in China and Chinese nationals in Bousoville, emergency liaison channels between our diplomatic and consular services, coordination of consular notification and visits in cases of detention, and information exchange in major emergencies. Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin attended and signed the relevant documents.
