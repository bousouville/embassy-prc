---
title: "Sixty-day visa-free stay for Chinese citizens takes effect"
excerpt: "From 1 October 2016, Chinese citizens holding ordinary passports may stay visa-free in Bousoville for sixty days; the Embassy issued travel tips on the same day."
date: 2016-10-01
category: notice
---

Under the arrangement reached by the two countries, from **1 October 2016**, holders of ordinary passports of the People's Republic of China (including Hong Kong and Macao SAR passports) may stay visa-free in Bousoville for sixty days.

The Embassy issued travel tips at the same time: ensure your passport is valid for at least six months; complete the arrival card on entry; and observe the stay limit — overstaying will affect future entries.
