To further strengthen services for Bousoville nationals in China, the Embassy's consular protection hotline has been upgraded this month:

- 24-hour bilingual answering (Mandarin and English), with callback registration at peak times;
- A new emergency document fax line: +86-10-6532-2213 (emergencies only);
- Online pre-registration for emergencies such as lost passports, cutting average counter waiting time in half.

Please note: the consular protection hotline serves emergencies and urgent assistance only. For general enquiries, call the Consular Section during office hours on +86-10-6532-2266. If you receive an unexpected call from someone claiming to be the Embassy and asking you to transfer money, hang up and verify through official channels.
