title: “Yuen Hoi Night” study fair held in Beijing
excerpt: The Embassy, with the University of Yuen Hoi and Nam Kong University of Technology, presented programmes, scholarships and the application process at a study fair in Beijing.
date: 2026-06-10
category: press
---

The Embassy held the “Yuen Hoi Night” study fair in Beijing today, with representatives of the University of Yuen Hoi and Nam Kong University of Technology presenting Bousoville's higher education to Chinese students.

Topics included programmes offered (law, business, marine studies, engineering and aviation, among others), the Traditional Chinese and English teaching environment, cost of living, scholarships and the student visa procedure.

The Cultural & Educational Section noted that educational exchanges between our countries have grown steadily in recent years, and that it has already assisted more than three hundred Chinese students with admission and visa matters this year. Further details are available in the “Culture & Education” section of this website.
