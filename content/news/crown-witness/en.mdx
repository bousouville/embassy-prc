date: 2026-08-25
category: announcement
---

“Crown Witness” is the national attestation provided by the Republic of Bousoville for important documents and contracts: verified and lodged under the witness of the Crown to the social contract, with its effect and preservation guaranteed by the State.

From **1 September 2026** the Embassy formally accepts enquiries and application forwarding from applicants in China:

1. Book and upload the document inventory through the Bousoville “Citizen & Enterprise Services” online portal;
2. The Consular Section verifies eligibility and documents;
3. The Embassy forwards the application to the Crown Witness Office in Yuen Hoi to arrange the witnessing procedure;
4. The attested document is returned to the applicant through the Consular Section.

Processing normally takes fifteen working days, with an express channel available. For enquiries, write to crown-witness@bousoville.hk.cn or call the Consular Section on +86-10-6532-2266 during office hours.
