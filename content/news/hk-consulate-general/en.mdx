---
title: "Consulate in Hong Kong reorganised as Consulate-General"
excerpt: "From 1 July 1997 the Consulate in Hong Kong becomes the Consulate-General in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, continuing to serve cross-border exchanges."
date: 1997-07-03
category: press
---

With effect from 1 July 1997, the Bousoville Consulate in Hong Kong was reorganised as the Consulate-General of the Republic of Bousoville in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

In the preceding months, the Embassy and the Consulate held several rounds of consultations on the reorganisation and the transfer of records, ensuring uninterrupted visa, authentication and passport services during the transition. The Consulate-General will continue to provide consular services to Hong Kong residents and travellers, in support of the Special Access arrangements for the Hong Kong SAR.
