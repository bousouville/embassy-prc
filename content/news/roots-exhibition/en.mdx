---
title: "“Not Far from Our Roots” exhibition opens in Beijing"
excerpt: "The ‘Not Far from Our Roots’ exhibition opened in Beijing, showing joint creations by artists of both countries on the themes of port cities and the memory of home."
date: 2019-10-15
category: press
---

The “Not Far from Our Roots” exhibition, co-organised by the Embassy and the Yuen Hoi Culture Week committee, opened in Beijing today.

Taking port cities and the memory of home as its theme, the exhibition presents joint creations in painting, photography and installation by artists of both countries, and runs for one month. Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin said at the opening that the exhibition extends the Yuen Hoi Culture Week, and that he hopes more Chinese audiences will come to know Bousoville's cultural life.
