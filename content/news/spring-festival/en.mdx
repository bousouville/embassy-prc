---
title: "Notice: consular hours during the Spring Festival"
excerpt: The Consular Section will be closed from 16 to 24 February 2026 for the Chinese New Year; the period around the holiday is busy, so please plan visa and notarisation matters early.
date: 2026-02-05
category: notice
---

The 2026 Chinese New Year falls on 17 February. The Consular Section's public counter hours will be adjusted as follows:

- **Closed**: Monday 16 February to Tuesday 24 February;
- **Resumes** on Wednesday 25 February (09:00–12:00, 13:30–17:00);
- The consular protection hotline (+86-10-6532-2212) continues to operate 24 hours a day throughout the holiday.

Visitors planning visa, passport, notarisation or authentication matters around the holiday are advised to book early and avoid peak times; the periods before and after the holiday are the busiest, so please allow ample time.

The Embassy staff wish our friends in both countries a prosperous New Year and a peaceful home.
