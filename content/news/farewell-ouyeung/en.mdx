---
title: "Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong bids farewell"
excerpt: "Before leaving after a decade in Beijing, Ambassador Au Yeung paid farewell calls on Chinese government departments and the diplomatic corps, which praised his work for the relationship."
date: 2007-03-15
category: press
---

Ambassador Au Yeung Chun-pong has concluded his ten-year term as Ambassador to China, paying farewell calls on Chinese government departments and the diplomatic corps in Beijing.

Ambassador Au Yeung took office in the year of Hong Kong's return. During his decade here, the relationship saw milestones including the renewal of the civil-aviation agreement, the opening of the new Chancery, the launch of the official website and the trade-and-investment dialogue. Chinese officials spoke highly of his contribution to the development of bilateral relations.
