---
title: "Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin departs Beijing"
excerpt: "Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin has left Beijing after an eighteen-year term, awarded the National Medal of Merit; before leaving he said the relationship had entered a mature stage."
date: 2025-04-15
category: press
---

Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin has concluded his eighteen-year term and departed Beijing.

Ambassador Tsang arrived in 2007. During his term the consular protection mechanism was established, the visa-free stay for Chinese citizens took effect, and cultural, educational and trade exchanges developed steadily; during the 2020 public-health emergency, the two countries remained in close coordination on travel. Before his departure, Ambassador Tsang said: “Our relationship has entered a mature stage — what concerns me more is how it can come closer to our two peoples.”

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced that Ambassador Tsang was awarded the National Medal of Merit before his departure. His successor, Ambassador Shum Kwok-wai, was appointed in March 2025.
