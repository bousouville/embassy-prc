(Reprinted from the notice of the Government of the Republic of Bousoville) The Republic of Bousoville and the Republic of Levizel signed a joint communiqué in Yuen Hoi, establishing diplomatic relations at ambassadorial level with immediate effect.

The two sides agreed to mutual visa-free entry of ninety days for ordinary passports, to open consultations on a Special Access regime, and to strengthen cooperation in search and rescue and sea-lane safety in the southern seas. The Republic of Levizel lies in the southern-sea region; its official languages are Simplified Chinese (mandarin) and English.

This Embassy reproduces the notice for information. For travel arrangements, please see the “Consular Services” section.
