---
title: "Civil-aviation authorities reach consensus on expanding the direct route and introducing wide-body aircraft"
excerpt: "At a working meeting in Beijing, the civil-aviation authorities of the two countries reached consensus on traffic rights, slot coordination and interline arrangements for scheduled direct flights."
date: 2024-08-21
category: press
---

The civil-aviation authorities of the two countries held a working meeting in Beijing today and reached practical consensus on expanding the Yuen Hoi—Beijing direct route and introducing a new wide-body aircraft type:

- The two sides agreed to increase passenger frequencies to six per week from August 2026, operated by wide-body aircraft;
- Arrangements were made for slot coordination between the capitals' airports and for interline baggage handling;
- China's civil-aviation authority confirmed that mutual recognition of airworthiness data will follow the 2001 civil-aviation agreement.

The expanded service is planned to begin on 5 August 2026. Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin attended and addressed the meeting.
