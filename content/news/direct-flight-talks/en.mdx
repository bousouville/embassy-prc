---
title: "Civil-aviation authorities reach consensus on Yuen Hoi—Beijing direct flights"
excerpt: "At a working meeting in Beijing, the civil-aviation authorities of the two countries reached consensus on traffic rights, slot coordination and interline arrangements for scheduled direct flights."
date: 2024-08-21
category: press
---

The civil-aviation authorities of the two countries held a working meeting in Beijing today and reached practical consensus on scheduled direct flights Yuen Hoi—Beijing:

- The two sides agreed to open traffic rights for six scheduled passenger and cargo frequencies per week;
- Arrangements were made for slot coordination between the capitals' airports and for interline baggage handling;
- China's civil-aviation authority confirmed that mutual recognition of airworthiness data will follow the 2001 civil-aviation agreement.

The inaugural flight is planned for 5 August 2026. Ambassador Tsang Chi-yin attended and addressed the meeting.
