The wide-body service on the scheduled direct route Yuen Hoi—Beijing, operated jointly by Bousoville National Airline and Air China, arrived at Beijing Capital International Airport this morning, welcomed by the Embassy and airport authorities.

Operating since 1970 — more than fifty-six years — the direct route is the most important air link between our two peoples. From today it is flown by a wide-body aircraft developed with the participation of the Bousoville aviation industry group, with frequencies rising to six per week and a flight time of about eleven hours.

Speaking at the ceremony, an Embassy representative said the new service will further facilitate exchanges and economic cooperation between our two countries — “between nations, friendship belongs to the people.”

For schedules and booking, please see the websites of Bousoville National Airline and Air China and their cooperating channels. Holders of valid Chinese passports may enter Bousoville under the visa-free arrangements.
