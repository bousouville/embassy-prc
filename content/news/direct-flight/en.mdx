title: Inaugural scheduled direct flight Yuen Hoi—Beijing
excerpt: Bousoville Airlines' first scheduled direct flight from Yuen Hoi arrived in Beijing this morning; the service operates six times a week with a flight time of about eleven hours.
date: 2026-08-05
category: press
---

The inaugural scheduled direct flight of Bousoville Airlines from Yuen Hoi to Beijing landed at Beijing Capital International Airport this morning, welcomed by the Embassy and airport authorities.

The route operates six times a week with a flight time of about eleven hours, flown by a wide-body aircraft developed jointly with the Bousoville aviation industry group. Speaking at the ceremony, an Embassy representative said the new service will greatly facilitate exchanges and economic cooperation between our two peoples — “between nations, friendship belongs to the people.”

For schedules and booking, please see the Bousoville Airlines website and cooperating carriers. Holders of valid Chinese passports may enter Bousoville under the visa-free arrangements.
