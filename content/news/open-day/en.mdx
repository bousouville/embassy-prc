This year marks the 65th anniversary of the Embassy and the 66th anniversary of diplomatic relations. The Embassy will hold an Open Day on **Thursday 17 September 2026**, from 09:00 to 16:00.

Programme highlights: an archives exhibition on the history of the Embassy since 1961, consular clinics (visas, passports, notarisation and authentication), a Bousoville cultural performance and a tourism information desk.

Entry is by appointment. Please register by 10 September by email (embassy@prc.embassy.bousoville.hk.cn) and bring valid identification on the day.
