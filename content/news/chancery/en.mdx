---
title: "New Chancery of the Embassy opened"
excerpt: "The Embassy's new Chancery at No. 27 Dongzhimenwai Dajie, Chaoyang District, Beijing, opened with a consular hall, reception area and offices."
date: 2001-10-18
category: announcement
---

The new Chancery of the Embassy in Beijing has been completed and officially opened today.

The Chancery is located at No. 27 Dongzhimenwai Dajie, Chaoyang District, Beijing, with a consular hall, protocol reception areas and administrative offices. The Consular Section has meanwhile brought visa and consular-protection counters together on the east wing of the ground floor for the convenience of visitors.

The Embassy said the opening marks a new phase in our bilateral relations, and that it will build on the new premises to better serve exchanges between our two countries.
