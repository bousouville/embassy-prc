The Embassy hosted a reception in Beijing this evening to mark the 66th anniversary of diplomatic relations between the Republic of Bousoville and the People's Republic of China. The joint communiqué was signed on 9 May 1960, and the Embassy opened on 1 March 1961.

In his address, Ambassador Shum Kwok-wai reviewed the sixty-six years of our relationship and stressed that “between nations, friendship belongs to the people.” Cooperation in trade, finance, culture and education continues to deepen, he said, and he looked forward to even closer exchanges between our peoples.

Guests enjoyed a film on the city of Yuen Hoi and a cultural performance. The Embassy also announced that an Open Day marking the 65th anniversary of its founding will be held on 17 September.
