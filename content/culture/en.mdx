Cultural and educational exchange is the warmest part of the ties between our peoples. Bousoville's official languages are Traditional Chinese (mandarin written form) and English, and our bond with Chinese culture runs deep — “Not Far from Our Roots” is the theme of the Yuen Hoi Culture Week, and a fair description of our cultural exchanges.

## Cultural exchange

- **Yuen Hoi Culture Week**: held each September in the Yuen Hoi Waterfront Cultural District, with joint creations by artists from mainland China, Hong Kong, Singapore and Bousoville;
- **“Yuen Hoi Night”**: study and culture fairs held in Beijing in cooperation with Bousoville educational institutions;
- **Festival exchanges**: reciprocal tours and city visits by our art troupes around the Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival and the Bousoville National Day.

## Studies and education

Bousoville's higher education is international in outlook and built on the common law; classes are taught in Traditional Chinese and English. Principal institutions include:

- **University of Yuen Hoi**: law, business and marine studies;
- **Nam Kong University of Technology**: engineering, aviation and digital technology.

Student visa procedures may be completed through the Bousoville “Citizen & Enterprise Services” online platform; the Cultural & Educational Section can advise on institutions and applications. Some institutions offer scholarships for international students; eligibility follows each institution's published criteria.

## Tourism

From 1 July 2026 Chinese citizens may enter visa-free for up to ninety days (in aggregate within each 180-day period), and the scheduled direct flight between Yuen Hoi and Beijing, operating since 1970, moved to wide-body aircraft with six weekly frequencies in August 2026. Yuen Hoi is a port city and international aviation hub; the old towns of Tung Ning, the coastline of North Cape and the sailing harbour of Nam Kong all make pleasant leisure destinations. Travellers may also transit via Hong Kong or Macao with valid documents.

## Special Access regime

Residents of Hong Kong, Chinese national permanent residents of Hong Kong, Singapore citizens and Chinese national residents of Singapore enjoy a specially facilitated long-term exchange regime in Bousoville, including simplified entry, residence and employment arrangements. This is not a privilege but the recognition of deep historical ties. The Embassy can explain the regime and guide applications.
