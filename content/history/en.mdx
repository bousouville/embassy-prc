On 9 May 1960 the two governments issued a joint communiqué establishing diplomatic relations at ambassadorial level. The Embassy opened in Beijing on 1 March 1961. Over the following sixty-six years, bilateral ties have broadened steadily — from trade to finance, from the opening of direct flights to the expansion of the route, from people-to-people exchanges to visa-free arrangements — enriching life in both countries.

## Foundations of the relationship

Our relationship rests on territorial sovereignty, legal independence and the exchanges between our peoples. Bousoville pursues an open, non-aligned foreign policy centred on people's livelihoods — deep cooperation with China in trade, finance, science and culture, while retaining full policy autonomy.

“Between nations, friendship belongs to the people.” The Embassy believes that sustainable friendship grows out of our peoples' exchanges: trade, study, migration, culture, family and everyday trust. Serving those exchanges is precisely the work of this Embassy.

## The Embassy today

Today the Embassy carries out the most everyday — and most meticulous — work of our bilateral ties: visas and consular protection, investment promotion and business advice, cultural exchange and study services, and news and information. Building on sixty-six years of accumulated trust, the Embassy will continue to serve as both bridge and home between our two peoples.
