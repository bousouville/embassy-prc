## Privacy

This website respects your privacy. It sets **no** tracking scripts, statistics code or advertising tags; the only client-side storage is your language preference (the “bousoville-lang” cookie and local storage) and a visitor counter (local storage, used only to display a visit number — nothing is transmitted).

The “Leave a message” feature does not transmit data through the website: clicking “Compose email” simply opens your email client on your own device. The Embassy receives no form submission.

To reach any section, please use the email addresses and telephone numbers published on the “Contact Us” page.
