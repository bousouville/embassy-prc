Economic and trade cooperation with mainland China is a cornerstone of our bilateral relationship. The Bousoville Dollar (BOU) is freely convertible, and Yuen Hoi — international airport and deep-water port — serves as a hub for our two supply chains. In 2025, two-way goods trade reached approximately USD 8.7 billion, up about 12 per cent year on year; two-way investment is growing steadily across aviation, ports, finance, information technology and green industry.

## Main areas of cooperation

| Area | Cooperation |
| --- | --- |
| Aviation & supply chains | Cooperation with China's commercial aircraft industry; mutual recognition of airworthiness data and maintenance training; direct scheduled flights Yuen Hoi—Beijing (from August 2026) |
| Finance & payments | Bilateral cooperation between financial institutions; interconnection testing completed between Bousoville's own clearing system, Clean, and China's principal clearing networks, sharply shortening cross-border settlement |
| Ports & logistics | Exchange on deep-water port and port-management experience; transoceanic freight and multimodal transport |
| Technology & digital | Enterprise cooperation in digital infrastructure, data-protection standards and cybersecurity |
| Green industry | Renewable energy, green building and low-carbon shipping under the green-finance framework |

## Investment promotion

Common fields for Chinese investors in Bousoville include port and airport support services, aviation components, IT services, hotels and retail, and renewables. The Commercial Section can arrange meetings with the investment-promotion authorities of Yuen Hoi, provide policy and legal guidance, and help you book government services.

## Exports and market access

Bousoville remains open to international trade: market-access arrangements follow the framework of the World Trade Organization, and customs operate on international commodity classification with single-window electronic declaration. Food exports such as poultry and aquatic products must meet sanitary and phytosanitary requirements; the Commercial Section is happy to advise on inspection and quarantine standards.
